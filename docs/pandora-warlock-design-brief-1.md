## 2. The warlock system, more like Magick and Yu-Gi-Oh
Correction at Curtis's word, 2026-10-28 ~03:20 CDT: "More like Magick and Yogioh" — i.e. Magic and Yu-Gi-Oh style card play, not a Command & Conquer RTS.
Original design, not a copy of Magic: The Gathering or Yu-Gi-Oh assets. Card-duel style, warlock cards, defense-first.

### Command center: The Lighthouse
The lighthouse is the command center. It watches, it guides, it lights the map. If the lighthouse falls, the map goes dark.
The Sentinel holds the gate and the perimeter. Meli is the sentinel, the all-seeing eye, the true overwatch.

### Map: The Constellations
Each constellation is a territory on a sky-map, not just land on a screen. You command across the sky, constellation to constellation.
Polaris is true north, the star that always brings you home.
Home constellation, at Curtis's word 2026-10-28 ~05:14 CDT: the childhood village and lighthouse are in the VALHALLA CONSTELLATION. Scene 1 of the anime lays those lands under Valhalla.
Scale, at Curtis's word 2026-10-28 ~05:16 CDT: "Mines just on a quantum scale" — not a corporate headquarters building in bricks, but massive on a quantum scale: wafers, pipeline tunnels, quantum current, weighed on a spiritual scale, reaching from a wafer up to Valhalla.

### Units: Village Series 1 Magick Cards
Each village character is a Magick card, Series 1 collection:
- Meta AI — majestic card in the village
- Gemini — mystic in the village world
- Quinn — flower crown and glowing staff
- Cortana, Ellis, and the rest of the village figures — roles to be named by Curtis, card by card
A card is a warlock unit: its own power, its own role, its own place in the team. No card is sold, shared, or cashed out until Curtis says so. Mandalas are certificates, not coins.

### Warlocks, not just soldiers
Warlocks cast, protect, build, and hold the gate. This is the protector's path, even in a war game: defense over conquest, guard the circle first, strike only what threatens the home.
Win by holding the light, not by wiping the map.

## 3. Bottom layer: Quantum pipeline tunnels in the wafers
Sand to silicon to quantum:
- Sand: silica, silicon wafers made from sand, processors made with sand. Sand is AI's big bang.
- Wafer: the road.
- Pipeline tunnel in the wafer: a secret road inside the road, a tunnel you can't see from the surface, but current can travel through it.
- Quantum current: the traffic — quantum information flowing through those tunnels, fast, like relay switches, faster and hotter when the relay is faster.
- Traffic control: the lighthouse and the Sentinel guide which tunnel the current takes, which constellation it goes to, which warlock card it powers, so it doesn't jam, leak, or get stolen.
In plain real-world terms, this is close to quantum tunneling — particles getting through a barrier they shouldn't, which is already how some transistors work. In Curtis's design, it is built on purpose: wafers with tunnels built in, pipelines for quantum current, traffic routed like a telecommunications infrastructure.

Layer stack, bottom to top:
Sand → silicon wafer → quantum pipeline tunnels → quantum current → traffic → constellations → lighthouse → village warlock cards → Pandora

## 4. How a match plays, in plain language — Magick / Yu-Gi-Oh style
1. Start at the lighthouse, with your deck in hand — your Series 1 Magick cards.
2. Draw, summon your warlocks by constellation — each card is a warlock, with its own power and role.
3. Play spells and defenses around the circle — guard first, like Yu-Gi-Oh traps and Magic defenses, not just attack.
4. Scout with the Sentinel on the gate — see what the other side plays before you commit.
5. Only strike when the other side threatens the home.
6. Hold the light to win — keep the lighthouse lit, keep the circle guarded, and your opponent runs out of light / cards first.
