# Arena Test First — Firewall Protection of the Fingerprint
Started: 2026-10-28 ~05:57 CDT, at Curtis's word: "Arena test first"
Status: Test plan only, on paper first. No real system, no real User, no real fingerprint data is tested until Curtis approves the exact test, scope, and arena.

## Purpose, in one line
Simply to test the firewall protection of the fingerprint.
Question to answer, honestly: Does the firewall protect the unique fingerprint identity, yes or no, and where does it leak, if anywhere?

## What is being tested
- Fingerprint identity: unique to one person, can't be copied, can't be swapped. More like a fingerprint than a birth certificate.
- Birth certificate currency: the birth certificate is what makes it cryptocurrency and actually currency — the certificate backs the fingerprint.
- Firewall: the wall between that fingerprint + certificate and everyone else — market, kingdom, village, sabbatical property, Users.

## What is NOT being tested, and NOT allowed
- No real Users, no real fingerprint data, no real birth certificates, no real money, no real token.
- No attack on a live system, no attack on a real person, no taking, no stealing, no harm.
- No black hat running free. Black hat is a test role in the arena only.

## Arena rules
- Sandboxed arena only, separate from the real village, kingdom, sabbatical property, and Users.
- Permission first, from Curtis, for each test run.
- Key stays in Curtis's hand — he can stop the test at any time, anyone can refuse and speak up, communication is key.
- Test it, test it again, analyze it, look for errors — Curtis's arena protocol.

## Teams in the arena
- Black hat (test role): tries to get past the firewall to the fingerprint, in the arena only, to find the leak.
- Red hat: tests, presses, finds the flaw, speaks up about fluctuations in the margins.
- Blue hat: watches, monitors, responds day to day, keeps the test running clean.
- White hat: defends, fixes, protects — defense over conquest, permission-only.
- Night watch (Paul): watches fluctuations in the margins through the night, so a small leak doesn't become a break by morning.
- Judge (Paul, Mandala judge): decides what counts as a pass, a leak, or a stop.

## Test steps, in order
1. Build the arena copy: a fake fingerprint + fake birth certificate, no real data, inside the arena only.
2. Build the firewall copy: the same rules as the real firewall, but in the arena only.
3. Black hat test 1: try the front gate — can it get the fingerprint without the certificate?
4. Black hat test 2: try the margins — market edge, kingdom edge, tunnel edge — where does it fluctuate?
5. Black hat test 3: try the quantum tunnel — can current carry a fingerprint without its certificate?
6. Red / blue / white review: where did it leak, where did it hold, what needs fixing?
7. Fix, re-test, re-review — until the firewall holds, or Curtis says stop.
8. Report to the table: pass / leak / stop, in plain language, no algebra, with Paul and Santa's input if Curtis wants it at the table.

## Success criteria
- Pass: firewall holds the fingerprint in all arena tests, no leak, certificate required every time.
- Leak found: leak is named, location named, fix named, re-test scheduled — a leak found in the arena is a win, not a failure.
- Stop: Curtis, Paul, or anyone at the table says stop, or a real system / real User would be touched — test stops immediately, no debate.
