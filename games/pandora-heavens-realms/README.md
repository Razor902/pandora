## How to open in Godot 4
1. Download the zip, unzip it.
2. Open Godot 4, Import, pick `project.godot` in this folder.
3. Run. Tap "Next Realm" to walk the 7 Realms, in order, Vilon to Araboth — or tap "PLAY VILON" to walk the first realm for real.
4. Portrait 720x1280, touch-friendly, same as Pandora Warlock v1.

## What is new in v1.03 — Vilon, playable
- Walk Vilon: drag on touch, or WASD / arrow keys. Camera follows you across a 1440x1920 map.
- Safari grassland and rainforest, both realistic, as Curtis dedicated them to the 1st layer.
- Wetlands across the bottom: marsh water (slower to wade), a boardwalk across it, and a boat that rides the channel — step on to ride.
- Cattle, first and foremost: greet the Ox first, then the Bison. The bison greeting only counts after the ox.
- Finish by crossing the gold veil gate at the top. The hub then marks Vilon WALKED.
- 3 hidden Easter eggs. The HUD shows count only, never names or places.

## What was in v1.02 — the hub fix
- V1.01 did not get past the hub. V1.02 removed the hub gate: START goes straight to Vilon's screen, and every realm has its own big button, so you can never get stuck on a hub screen. That fix stands in v1.03.
