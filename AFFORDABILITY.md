## The cost-down strategies (why this works)

1. **Never build the headset.** Meta sells the Quest 3S near cost to own the platform. The single biggest cost-down lever is refusing to build custom displays, optics, or silicon — buy the commodity headset and put the budget into the box's soul.
2. **The phone as the computer.** At the entry tier, compute costs $0 — the family's phone does it all.
3. **Commodity safety.** The entire biometric cuff — the thing that keeps the box safe — is under $20 in parts. Safety was never the expensive part.
4. **Open-source everything.** Unreal (free to start), Python, Linux, PythonX: the software bill is $0.
5. **The refurbished market.** Last year's headset is this year's people's build.
6. **The mandala economy.** Inside the box, money isn't money — prove yourself, earn a mandala, show it for one thing. The in-world economy runs on proof-of-self, so the world inside stays cheap forever.

## Honest headwinds

- **Memory prices are rising.** The 2026 chip shortage pushed the Quest 3S from $299.99 to $349.99 and the Pi 5 2GB from $50 to $77.50. Affordability is a moving target; the tiers above are October 2026 truth.
- **5K is not affordable.** No 5K VR path exists under ~$900+ (headset alone, before the PC). 5K stays on the dream horizon; the people's build prototypes at lower resolutions and scales up later.
- **This is a plan, not a product.** No box is for sale. No prices are promised. Verify everything before spending a dollar.
